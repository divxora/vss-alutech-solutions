Aluminium Extrusion Press Solutions & Machinery

Planning a new aluminium extrusion plant or upgrading an existing production line? VSS Alutech Solutions provides end-to-end machinery consultancy to ensure you invest in the right press capacity and technology.

The extrusion press is the beating heart of your plant. Choosing the appropriate tonnage, container size, hydraulic architecture, and runout handling system dictates your product profile capability, cycle speed, and operational profitability for decades.

●
Extrusion Press Selection: Front-loading, back-loading, short-stroke, and direct extrusion press evaluation tailored to your target product range.
●
Capacity & Tonnage Consultation: Press sizing analysis (from 550 UST up to 4000+ UST) based on billet diameter, profile CCD, wall thickness, and alloy mix (6063, 6061, 6082).
●
Machinery Sourcing & Quotations: Vendor comparison, commercial quotation analysis, and technical vetting across domestic and international machinery makers.
●
Technical Specifications & Line Automation: Reviewing hydraulic pumps, proportional valves, PLC control systems, runout cooling tables, pullers, and stretcher specifications.
●
Installation & Commissioning Coordination: Foundation layout planning, civil coordination, mechanical assembly oversight, hydraulic flushing, and trial extrusion support.
●
New Plant Project Consultation: Complete greenfield and brownfield master planning, plant civil footprint sizing, crane capacities, and utility load assessments.

SERVICE CATEGORY 02

Industrial Furnace Solutions for Aluminium Extrusion

Thermal management directly determines metal metallurgical properties, surface finish, extrusion speed, and fuel consumption. VSS Alutech Solutions assists plants in planning and sourcing robust thermal systems.

Whether you require rapid heating for high-volume architectural runs or tightly controlled temperature gradients for aerospace-grade alloys, we help identify the most energy-efficient, reliable furnace technology.

●
Billet Heating Furnaces: Sourcing guidance for continuous multi-log gas heaters with integrated hot log shears, or induction heaters providing taper heating for isothermal extrusion.
●
Ageing Ovens (T4, T5, T6 Tempers): Design evaluation for batch-type ageing ovens featuring high-velocity cross-flow recirculation fans and tight temperature uniformity (+/- 3°C).
●
Melting & Holding Furnaces: Consultation on reverberatory melting furnaces, scrap recycling setups, and holding furnaces where casting or remelt is integrated.
●
Furnace Selection & Capacity Planning: Matching hourly thermal throughput (kg/hr) to press cycle demand to avoid furnace bottlenecks or idle waiting time.
●
Equipment Sourcing & Energy Efficiency: Vetting burner systems, recuperators, refractory linings, and flue gas heat recovery systems to drive down specific fuel cost per MT.
●
Installation & Project Execution Coordination: Vendor schedule tracking, refractory drying cycles, combustion tuning, and thermal profile compliance verification.

SERVICE CATEGORY 03

Plant Troubleshooting & Operational Consultancy

Targeted for existing aluminium extrusion plants dealing with production slowdowns, frequent die chokes, high scrap rates, or surface quality rejects.

Extrusion involves dynamic interplay between alloy temperature, container friction, die bearing friction, quench rate, and stretching tension. Our troubleshooting approach diagnoses root causes rather than symptoms.

●
Production Bottleneck Elimination: Dead-cycle time reduction, billet transfer optimization, puller return synchronization, and stretcher cycle balancing.
●
Process & Quality Defect Troubleshooting: Systematic elimination of surface tears, die lines, pick-up, blisters, grain boundary sensitization, cross-sectional distortion, and uneven wall thickness.
●
Equipment-Related Problems: Container alignment verification, stem deflection checks, platen parallelism, hydraulic valve response times, and temperature logging.
●
Technical Consultancy & Standard Operating Procedures: Implementing disciplined die maintenance, nitriding schedules, caustic wash guidelines, and billet temperature recipes.
●
Operational Improvement Programs: Scrap reduction strategies (butt length optimization, front/back saw cuts) to improve overall plant recovery rates.
●
Expert On-Site Visits & Contract Support: Hands-on plant audits, scheduled monthly reviews, and emergency breakdown advisory services.

COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear technical insights on our machinery consultancy, die sourcing workflow, plant troubleshooting, and manpower placement.

Clients share 2D DWG/DXF drawings, 3D STEP files, alloy requirements, and press specifications with us. We match the profile geometry with vetted, independent certified tool rooms across India that possess specialized wire EDM, CNC machining centers, and vacuum nitriding capabilities. VSS manages the design land calculations, commercial quotations, schedule expediting, and trial inspection reports — ensuring high-accuracy tooling without client trial-and-error.

For standard architectural profiles (doors, windows, curtain wall trims) using 6063 alloy, a 5-inch (127 mm) billet press rated at 1,100 UST or 7-inch (178 mm) press rated at 1,800 UST is typically ideal. Solar mounting rails and heavier structural channels frequently utilize 7-inch to 8-inch billet presses (1,800 UST to 2,500 UST) to allow multi-cavity extrusion with high recovery rates. Use our interactive Press Sizing Estimator above or consult our team for tonnage calculations based on your product mix.

Our plant troubleshooting covers both mechanical and metallurgical challenges: hydraulic pressure fluctuations, container misalignment, profile surface tearing, die lines, extrusion blisters, grain boundary sensitization, and quench table warping. We conduct on-site diagnostics, review log heating and ageing oven temperature profiles, and establish standard operating procedures (SOPs) to reduce scrap rates and boost recovery.

Industrial manpower is arranged on contract, project, or shift basis according to customer specifications and geographical location. Candidates undergo practical verification of their extrusion plant background (e.g. experience operating 1,100–3,000 UST presses, hot log shears, or ageing ovens). Placement is subject to local availability, candidate onboarding, and mutually agreed statutory compliance terms.
